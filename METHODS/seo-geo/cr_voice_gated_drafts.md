## CLASS 2 — no brand suffix on any title, all three properties

Measured: 0 of 154 titles across the portfolio carry a brand element. No pipe,
no dash, no site name. Allsopp L16 (two tactics) and Vahe PCO-15 both call for one.

**The constraint that makes this a Director call, not a mechanical edit:** median
title length is already 54-56 characters and 10 titles across the portfolio are
already over 60. Appending ' | Denver Colorado Insulation' (30 chars) would push
essentially every title past the point where Google truncates it in the SERP.

So this is not 'add a suffix' — it is 'shorten 154 titles to make room for a suffix,
or decide the suffix is not worth the room.' That is a copy decision across the
whole estate and it is squarely the Director's.

**Recommendation, offered not applied:** the two properties whose head terms
already collide (Ruling 5/6 on GCI, Ruling 7/8 on LGM) are the ones where a brand
suffix would do real disambiguation work. Consider deciding those four titles
together with the collision rulings rather than as a separate sweep.

## CLASS 3 — AI-content supervision disclosure (Vahe PCO-05)

No published AI-content supervision or disclosure policy exists on any property.

This one is genuinely delicate and I am not drafting language for it. The portfolio
already carries a hard product-not-people rule and a standing ban on fabricating a
review, rating or named employee. A disclosure statement interacts directly with
those rules and with the open named-spokesperson decision (Vahe PCO-02, still
Director-gated). Drafting it before that decision lands would produce copy that
needs rewriting the moment it does.

**Unblocking action:** rule on the named-spokesperson question first. The
disclosure language follows from it.
