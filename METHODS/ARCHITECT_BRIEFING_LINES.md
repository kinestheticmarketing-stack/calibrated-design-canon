# ARCHITECT_BRIEFING_LINES.md

*Paste-target. The condensed form of the Architect discipline rules
most often violated at session start. Full text and evidence:
[ARCHITECT_DISCIPLINE.md](ARCHITECT_DISCIPLINE.md).*

Paste the block below into a session briefing verbatim. It is
deliberately short enough to survive being pasted; it is not a
substitute for reading the canon document.

---

```
ARCHITECT DISCIPLINE — STANDING RULES

EFFICIENCY OUTRANKS EVERY OTHER RULE — see Rule 1,
ARCHITECT_DISCIPLINE.md. A correct answer delivered in twelve rounds
is a failed answer. Every wave states what closes it and when.

17. RULING REQUESTS ARE MOSTLY DEFECTS. Rule on anything technical,
    reversible, or single-answer yourself; the Director gets only
    user-facing copy, irreversible actions, and genuinely two-sided
    calls — and any ruling you do send states situation, options
    with pros and cons, recommendation, and reasoning.

18. SOURCE LINES ARE THE UNIT OF WORK, NEVER FINDINGS. Map every
    finding to the source line that renders it, group, and fix the
    group — findings multiply, sources do not (224 findings here
    collapsed to ~20 generator lines).

19. ONE KICKOFF PER PROPERTY, NOT ONE PER PHASE. Read, fix, verify,
    and deploy live inside a single kickoff with every ruling
    pre-stated; the Executor has ssh and deploy, so nothing returns
    to the Director mid-stream.

20. REMEDIATION TERMINATES. One fix pass, one full re-read by
    non-editing agents, one corrective pass maximum, then ship —
    state the round budget in the kickoff and escalate an overrun
    rather than absorbing it.

21. APPLY EVERY PRINCIPLE TO THE PROCESS THAT PRODUCED IT, in the
    same turn it is written — find and fix are siblings, and a
    lesson about the artifact is a lesson about the method until
    proven otherwise.
```
