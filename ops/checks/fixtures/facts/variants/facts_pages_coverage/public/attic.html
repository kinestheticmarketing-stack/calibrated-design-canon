Home Attic Insulation Rebates

Attic insulation

Attic insulation guidance for Northern Colorado's climate zone recommends a level between R49 and R60. (Example Building Codes Council)

The council's guidance puts it plainly: “Uninsulated attics in Climate Zone 5 should be brought to R49 to R60.”

Spray foam at the attic floor

Closed-cell spray foam cut measured air leakage through the attic floor by about half (Example Building Science Lab) in most tested homes.

Keep occupants out of the house for at least 24 hours after spray foam is applied.
