How Do I Stack the Whole Home Efficiency Bonus on Top of the Combo Bonus?

The Whole Home Efficiency Bonus layers on top of the Combo Bonus, and Xcel Energy adds a bonus on top of the Whole Home Efficiency Bonus.

You can layer the federal 25C credit on top of the Whole Home Efficiency Bonus for further coverage, so you can stack rebates instead of leaving money on the table.

The federal 25C credit will not stack on top of the Whole Home Efficiency Bonus.
