According to Xcel Energy’s residential rebate summary, the qualifying minimum standard for the air sealing rebate is a 20% reduction in CFM 50.

Because the air sealing rebate turns on a 20% reduction in CFM 50, the blower door reading is what decides whether the work qualifies.

The Whole Home Efficiency Bonus adds 25% on all standard rebates when three or more measures are installed within two years of enrolling.
