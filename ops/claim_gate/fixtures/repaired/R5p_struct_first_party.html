The qualifying threshold

Air sealing is a separate measure and requires a blower door test showing at least a 20% reduction in leakage.

Attic insulation is rebated at 50% of project cost, and air sealing separately where a blower door documents a 20% leakage reduction.

The incentive is capped at 75% and the air sealing tier begins at a measured reduction of 20 percent.
