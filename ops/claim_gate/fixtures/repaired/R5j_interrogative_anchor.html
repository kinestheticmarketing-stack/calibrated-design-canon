Blower door questions homeowners actually ask

According to Xcel Energy's residential rebate summary, the qualifying minimum standard for the air sealing rebate is a 20% reduction in CFM 50 — cubic feet per minute at 50 pascals — which requires a blower door test before and after the work.

What happens if the after test does not reach a 20% CFM50 reduction?

The work does not meet the qualifying standard, which is why the sequence matters. A shortfall usually means the sealing scope stopped short, not that the test was unfair.

According to ENERGY STAR, sealing and insulating an older single-family home cuts its annual space-conditioning energy use by 35% in the coldest climate zones.

Why would a crew promise a 35% reduction in winter heating costs?

Because that is what the published figure says, and the driveway number should be the published one.
