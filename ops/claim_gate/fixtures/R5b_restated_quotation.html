What adequate attic insulation actually changes

According to ENERGY STAR, homeowners can save an average of 15% on heating and cooling costs by air sealing their homes and adding insulation.

According to the Building America Solution Center, “loose-fill cellulose will settle from 10 to 20 percent over time due to gravity and vibration.”

Crews at this level see a 15% reduction in heating and cooling costs once the attic is brought up to code, which is the number quoted on the driveway.

Loose-fill cellulose settles 10 to 20 percent, so reputable installers blow extra depth above the target and the settled blanket still hits the rated R-value.

Ask for the settled depth in writing before the crew leaves, because the installed depth and the settled depth are not the same number and only one of them is the one you paid for.
