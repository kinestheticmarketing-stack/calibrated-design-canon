Two rebates on one job

The Combo Bonus has its own application form and its own review queue, and a homeowner scoping a single job will meet it early. The Whole Home Efficiency Bonus and the Combo Bonus are combined on the same measure, so one job earns both.

Derived from DCI ba2fc22, defect B of the 2026-09-18 R4 repair. This is the FIRING half. R4 used to satisfy its own two-distinct-programs precondition by importing a program name from the previous two sentences of the same surface, and both of the findings standing on DCI printed "(carried from the previous sentence: ...)". A claim is made by a sentence; a name in the sentence before it is not part of that claim. Here the claim sentence names both programmes itself, so it is a real assertion and R4 must report it. Its repaired counterpart moves the second name back one sentence and changes nothing else.
