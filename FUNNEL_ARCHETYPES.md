# Funnel Archetypes

*Portfolio-level canon. A library of proven funnel / business-model
archetypes for the Kinesthetic Marketing portfolio. Each archetype is
both a live income asset and a reference implementation to clone or
sell. Every entry is meant to be battle-tested by building a real
asset before it becomes a productized pattern.*

*Established 2026-06-30.*

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHY THIS EXISTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These are not separate projects. They are structural templates,
differentiated on the axis that matters — how the asset is monetized
and how intake works — not by vertical. A digital insulation site and
a digital plumbing site are the same archetype; DCI and the real-estate
hybrid are genuinely different archetypes. Collecting archetypes
compounds; collecting verticals only lengthens the list.

Three layers feed each other on every build:
  - ASSETS earn rent / revenue (income floor)
  - ARCHETYPES become the productized funnel library (sold, or deployed
    for clients by pulling the closest pattern and customizing the delta)
  - SKILLS (SEO/GEO, the build chassis, the four-role operating model)
    sharpen on every build (the capability that makes extension cheap)

Each new archetype advances all three at once.

THE ARCHETYPES

--- 1. DIGITAL RANK-AND-RENT ---
Live example:   Denver Colorado Insulation (denvercoloradoinsulation.com)
Status:         LIVE — priority asset
Intake:         Pure organic. SEO/GEO ranking; digital capture.
Follow-up:      Digital.
Monetization:   Own the ranked asset, rent the lead flow.
Skill sharpened: SEO/GEO, deterministic generator chassis.

--- 2. HYBRID PHYSICAL–DIGITAL RANK-AND-RENT ---
Concept example: Denver real-estate lead-gen (unbuilt)
Status:         QUEUED behind DCI reaching set-and-minimal-maintenance
Intake:         Physical mail as the intake VALVE (postcard / postal
                route), driving to a digital hook. Plus organic SEO/GEO.
Follow-up:      Digital, automated, long-horizon.
Monetization:   Own two nurtured lists, rent the output (flat
                exclusivity rent primary).
Skill sharpened: SEO/GEO + physical-channel intake + nurture automation.
Rationale:      Channel abandonment creates novelty → novelty earns
                attention → attention converts. Direct mail is under-used,
                so a physical piece is seen where digital is scrolled past.
                Requires competence in both mediums at once — itself a moat.

    ENGINE SKETCH (as resolved 2026-06-30):
    - Two front doors, same shape as the Kinesthetic two-doors-converging
      model:
        SELLER door (priority build, highest-value list):
          Hook "What's your [Neighborhood] home worth?" — address-based
          valuation. One closed seller = five-figure commission event.
        BUYER door (volume list):
          Hook "Get the [Neighborhood] hot-list — every home for sale,
          pictures + prices, updated weekly." Capture criteria.
    - The asset IS the two owned, nurtured lists (Dean Jackson model),
      not the site. The list is the equity. Site + postcard are intake
      valves.
    - Nurture is where value compounds — the multi-year follow-up every
      agent abandons after weeks. Automated market-updates + weekly
      criteria-matched hot-list + 9-word reactivation ("Are you still
      looking to [buy/sell] in [area]?"). n8n + SendGrid. Mostly passive
      once built. Leads never expire.
    - Chassis: DCI backend pattern (Express + Postgres + SendGrid + n8n).
      Two segments: sellers, buyers. Each lead a permanent tagged record.
    - MONETIZATION options, in order of preference:
        (a) Flat monthly exclusivity rent — cleanest, most passive,
            rank-and-rent-native. RECOMMENDED PRIMARY.
        (b) Per-lead media fee — caps upside, invites quality disputes;
            workable as a layer on a flat base.
        (c) Closing rev-share (% commission) — highest upside but legally
            sensitive: real-estate referral fees tied to closings
            generally must flow between LICENSED parties (RESPA-type
            constraints). Flat rent / per-lead media fee is the cleaner
            structure for an unlicensed asset owner. Not legal advice —
            attorney review before any rev-share; flat rent sidesteps most.
    - If a renter churns, the lists and ranking stay yours and keep
      compounding. Swap in the next agent.
    - MVP: seller engine first, single Denver farm (DCI proof-case logic),
      then bolt on the buyer door once the seller loop produces.

--- 3. AGENCY / PRODUCTIZED SERVICE ---
Live example:   Kinesthetic Marketing (funnel + Porter model)
Status:         Build/prove in progress
Intake:         Varies per client engagement.
Follow-up:      Service-delivered.
Monetization:   Labor-linked but productizable. Prove the funnel/Porter
                model on owned assets, then sell the model — or get hired
                to deploy it — for agencies and consultants.
Role in stack:  NOT competing with rank-and-rent for the primary-income
                slot. It is the proof engine and the productization path.
                Rank-and-rent is the income floor that buys selectivity
                on the agency side. One funds the lifestyle; the other
                monetizes the proof.
Skill sharpened: The whole four-role AI operating model — the case study
                for how few people it takes to run real businesses.

--- 4. E-COMMERCE / PHYSICAL PRODUCT ---
Concept example: Green River Formation fossils (Kemmerer, WY) (unbuilt)
Status:         FUTURE — after income floor + nomad freedom exist
Intake:         SEO/GEO collector- and gift-intent + product funnel.
Follow-up:      Digital + repeat purchase.
Monetization:   Physical product with a value-ladder progression (below).
New muscle:     Inventory/fulfillment logistics; later, photogrammetry /
                3D scanning.
Sourcing note:  Fee-dig quarries (~$150/day, chisel-what-you-carry,
                museum-grade reserved by quarry) = near-zero marginal
                cost on raw material. Heavy-but-cheap flips the usual
                weight economics: cheap input absorbs freight and still
                clears high margin.
Legal note:     Vertebrate fossils from FEDERAL land are restricted;
                invertebrate/plant fossils and anything from PRIVATE
                quarries (most commercial Green River trade) are generally
                fine to sell. Source through private fee-dig quarries.
                Not legal advice — verify before sourcing.

    VALUE-LADDER PROGRESSION (fossil instance; generalizes):
    Value climbs from labor-linked toward asset-linked, each layer more
    passive and higher-margin, each built on the one before it.
      (1) Raw material — near-free weekend sourcing.
      (2) Finished physical product — museum-style shadow box + placard
          (species, formation, ~50M-yr Eocene age, provenance). Sells into
          a higher tier with no comparison shopping; margin is in curation
          and assembly, not the rock. $20 commodity → $200 object.
      (3) Physical-digital hybrid — cheap limestone tile carrier with a
          laser-etched QR unlocking the AR/3D scan. Light, infinitely
          available; the value is the scan the tile unlocks, not the rock.
      (4) Pure digital good — 3D scan sold infinitely; only cost
          (source + scan) paid once. Same "own asset, sell output many
          times" logic as rank-and-rent, in a new medium.
      (5) Licensed library — once the scan library is deep, the collection
          itself is the product. License/rent to universities, schools,
          museums, ed-platforms; consumer AR app/subscription. Highest-
          margin, most-passive layer.
    Skill sharpened: photogrammetry/3D scanning transfers to any future
    product or client work.

CROSS-ARCHETYPE PATTERN — THE INTAKE-VALVE / OWNED-NURTURE MOVE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Surfaced from the Haven Lifestyles model (MFM transcript, 2026-06-30):
a $10M real-estate advertising magazine where ~90% of exposure had
already moved online while the print piece stayed expensive.

The reusable pattern — portfolio-level, not real-estate-specific:
  - The physical piece's ONLY job is intake. It is not the engine.
  - The engine and the follow-up live online, cheaply.
  - The money is in the nurture nobody else runs.

Generalizes across every rank-and-rent vertical. The seller/buyer list
structure is the real-estate instance; the underlying move —
postcard-as-valve, owned list as equity, automated long-horizon
follow-up as the moat — is canon.

Haven's own confessed weakness proves the point: 10,000+ advertisers a
year, most one-and-done, only ~1,500 on auto-debit. Fat front door, no
continuity layer. The nurture is the part nobody builds and the part
worth the most.

CRO PRINCIPLES, MAPPED BY ARCHETYPE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Source: Hormozi web-design/CRO advice (agency breakdown video,
2026-06-30). Most of it restates DR canon already in COPY_VOICE and the
copywriting skill. The upgrade is knowing which principles bind to which
archetype rather than applying a flat checklist.

UNIVERSAL (all archetypes):
  - Clarity beats pretty. A visitor must know what you do and the next
    step in ~5 seconds. Pretty that makes them work loses to plain-clear.
  - Above-the-fold is the only section every visitor is guaranteed to
    see. If one thing is right on the whole page, it's that.
  - Value equation runs the page: dream outcome, perceived likelihood,
    time, effort/sacrifice. Sell the end state, not the product.
  - Prioritize the BOTTOM of the equation. Belief and speed (proof,
    guarantee, page/comprehension speed) beat a bigger promise. When only
    two or three things fit above the fold, spend them on
    likelihood-and-speed before promise.
  - You sell against inaction and risk, not competitors. Lower the action
    threshold until clicking is the easy choice.
  - Cluster trust elements AT the CTA — proof adjacent to every button,
    not parked three scrolls down. Doubt spikes in the half-second before
    commit. Specific guarantees; the vague "satisfaction guaranteed" badge
    gets skipped.

HIGH-TRAFFIC ASSETS ONLY:
  - One-split-test-a-week cadence assumes traffic volume to reach
    significance. A thin-traffic rank-and-rent farm page CANNOT validate
    weekly A/B tests — it reads noise as signal. The discipline transfers
    (one deliberate change, learn one thing); the mechanism does not.
    On thin-traffic rank-and-rent, spend optimization budget on ranking,
    intake, and nurture — not hero A/B.

AGENCY ARCHETYPE ONLY:
  - Visual differentiation / distinctive point of view. "Look like every
    competitor, get priced like every competitor" is load-bearing for the
    premium Kinesthetic offer. INVERTED for rank-and-rent: commodity-
    clarity is fine and arguably preferred — a clean, trustworthy local
    lead-gen site IS the goal; a distinctive POV is wasted effort there.

BUILD-METHOD COUNTERMEASURE (all AI-generated builds):
  - Real, specific, imperfect imagery + local proof as the anti-templated
    signal. Deterministic AI-generated builds risk reading as fake ("what
    else here is fake?"). Real-human-touched imagery is a direct
    countermeasure to the failure mode this build method is prone to —
    not a nice-to-have.

DECISIONS LOCKED — 2026-06-30
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  - PREBUILD-THEN-RENT posture for the real-estate hybrid, matching DCI.
    Rationale: the build itself is the point — it sharpens the productized
    capability (the thing sold / hired for on the agency side). Preselling
    routes around the hard part. Prebuild → rank → nurture → then sign the
    renter. No LOI first.
  - PRIORITY: DCI is the live asset. All other archetypes are queued behind
    DCI reaching set-and-minimal-maintenance. No parallel builds — the
    rank-and-rent failure mode is collecting verticals faster than they're
    finished. Each finished asset relieves pressure on the next; each gets
    built from a calmer position than the last.
  - REAL-ESTATE MVP: seller engine first (highest-value list), single
    Denver farm, then buyer door.
